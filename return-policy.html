Returns,
Refunds & Exchanges

Every @TENACIOUS creation is made with intention. Our made-to-order production model allows us to produce each purchase through our fulfilment partner, Printful. This policy explains how we handle product concerns, returns, refunds and exchanges.

@TENACIOUS — RESILIENT BY CHOICE.

01. Our Made-to-Order Model

@TENACIOUS operates on a Print-on-Demand (POD) production model.

Products are manufactured after an order is placed and confirmed. Consequently, our collections are not managed like conventional ready-made retail inventory.

We encourage customers to carefully review product descriptions, sizing guides, colours and specifications before completing a purchase.

02. Change of Mind & Customer Preferences

Because our products are individually manufactured after purchase, we generally do not accept returns or exchanges for:

  • Change of mind after an order enters production.
  • Incorrect size selected by the customer.
  • Incorrect colour selected by the customer.
  • Personal preference.
  • Products that match their published specifications but do not meet a customer's personal expectations.

We therefore encourage customers to consult the available product information before ordering.

03. Damaged, Defective & Misprinted Products

We take product quality seriously. If an order arrives damaged, defective, misprinted or materially different from what was purchased, please contact our customer support team.

Eligible issues may include:

  • Manufacturing defects.
  • Printing errors.
  • Products damaged during delivery.
  • Incorrect products supplied.
  • Incorrect sizes supplied due to a fulfilment error.

Claims for eligible product issues should be submitted within 30 days of receiving the affected product.

Please provide your order number, a description of the issue and clear photographs showing the affected product.

Following an assessment with our fulfilment partner, an appropriate resolution may include a replacement or refund.

Where an issue is confirmed to be a manufacturing or fulfilment error, customers will not be charged for correcting that error.

04. Size & Colour Exchanges

Since our products are made to order, we generally do not offer exchanges for customer-selected sizes or colours.

Customers should consult individual product sizing charts and descriptions before purchasing.

However, if the incorrect size or colour was supplied because of a fulfilment error, please contact us for assistance.

05. Order Cancellations & Modifications

Orders enter production shortly after confirmation. Consequently, cancellation or modification requests must be submitted as soon as possible.

Once production has commenced, cancellation, modification or refund requests based solely on a change of mind may no longer be accommodated.

If you need to make changes to an order, contact our customer support team immediately.

06. Lost or Undelivered Orders

If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery timeframe, please contact us.

For packages confirmed as lost in transit, claims should be submitted within 30 days after the estimated delivery date.

We will investigate eligible claims with our fulfilment partner and determine an appropriate resolution.

07. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment channel, subject to the procedures of the relevant payment provider.

Refund processing and settlement times may vary depending on the payment method and financial institution.

Customers will be informed of the applicable resolution following the assessment of their claim.

08. Return Authorisation

Please do not independently send products to Printful or any other fulfilment facility.

If a physical return is required, our customer support team will provide the appropriate instructions and return arrangements.

09. Customer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or restrict consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable laws.

Where applicable consumer protection legislation provides customers with additional rights or remedies, those rights remain unaffected.

10. Contact & Customer Support

For returns, refunds, exchanges or product-related concerns, please contact:

Email: support@tenaciously.shop

Please include your order number and relevant photographs when reporting a product issue.
