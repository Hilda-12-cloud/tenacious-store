Shipping &
Delivery Policy

Every @TENACIOUS creation is produced with intention. Through our made-to-order production model and partnership with Printful, we bring our collections closer to customers across different destinations.

@TENACIOUS — RESILIENT BY CHOICE.

01. Order Processing & Production

@TENACIOUS operates on a Print-on-Demand (POD) model. This means products are manufactured after an order has been successfully placed rather than produced in advance for conventional inventory.

Our fulfilment partner, Printful, handles the production and preparation of eligible orders.

Production generally takes approximately 2–5 business days, although certain products, seasonal demand and operational circumstances may affect this timeframe.

Production time is separate from shipping and delivery time.

02. Shipping Charges

Please note that shipping is not free and is not included in the displayed product prices unless expressly stated otherwise.

Shipping charges are calculated separately and may depend on:

  • Delivery destination.
  • Product category and quantity.
  • Package weight and dimensions.
  • Available shipping methods.

Applicable shipping charges will be communicated to customers before payment is completed.

Customers are responsible for the shipping fees associated with their orders.

03. Shipping & Delivery Times

Delivery timelines vary depending on the destination, shipping method, product and fulfilment location.

Shipping estimates are separate from production time. Customers should consider both production and shipping when estimating their order's arrival.

Delivery estimates are not guaranteed. Circumstances such as customs inspections, public holidays, weather conditions and carrier disruptions may cause delays.

04. International Shipping

@TENACIOUS welcomes orders from eligible international destinations.

Through Printful's international fulfilment network, eligible products may be manufactured and dispatched from different fulfilment facilities depending on product availability and delivery destination.

Shipping availability varies by product and destination. Certain countries and regions may be subject to shipping restrictions.

05. Order Tracking

Once an order has been fulfilled and dispatched, tracking information will be provided where available.

Customers can use the tracking details to monitor their shipment and follow delivery updates.

Please allow time for tracking information to become active after dispatch.

06. Customs, Duties & Import Taxes

International shipments may be subject to customs duties, import taxes or additional handling charges imposed by the destination country.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, customers are responsible for applicable customs duties, taxes and import charges.

These charges are determined by the relevant authorities and are outside the direct control of @TENACIOUS.

07. Incorrect Delivery Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate and complete delivery information when placing an order.

Incorrect or incomplete addresses may result in delivery delays, failed deliveries or returned packages.

Additional shipping charges may apply where a new shipment is required because of incorrect information supplied by the customer.

08. Lost, Delayed or Undelivered Orders

If an order has not arrived within the estimated delivery timeframe, customers should contact our support team.

We will investigate eligible delivery issues with our fulfilment partner and work towards an appropriate resolution.

For packages confirmed as lost, please contact us within 30 days after the estimated delivery date.

09. Contact & Customer Support

For shipping enquiries, delivery concerns or order-related assistance, please contact:

Email: support@tenaciously.shop

Please include your order number when contacting our customer support team.
