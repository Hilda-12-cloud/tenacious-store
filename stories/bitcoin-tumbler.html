THE NEW CURRENCY.

Value Beyond the Visible.
Product: Bitcoin Stainless Steel Tumbler.

The world is constantly changing.

The ways we communicate, conduct business, exchange information and understand value have evolved considerably. What once depended almost entirely on traditional systems now exists alongside an increasingly digital landscape.

Among the most recognisable expressions of this transformation is Bitcoin.

More than a symbol associated with digital currency, its distinctive identity represents a significant conversation about technology, financial innovation and the changing relationship between people and value.

This idea inspired our THE NEW CURRENCY design.

We wanted to translate an element of contemporary financial culture into a visually striking everyday object.

The result is a design that combines the recognisable Bitcoin emblem with a sophisticated contrast of black, gold and white.

It is an expression of modern identity, technological influence and the visual language of a changing world.

THE EMBLEM: A SYMBOL OF CHANGE

At the centre of the artwork is the unmistakable Bitcoin symbol, presented in a prominent golden finish.

Its familiar form is enclosed within a bold circular composition, creating a visual focal point against the dark background.

The symbol is immediately recognisable, carrying associations with digital currency, decentralised technology and an evolving approach to financial exchange.

We appreciate the significance of symbols in contemporary culture. A single emblem can communicate an entire subject, introduce a conversation or represent an idea that extends beyond its physical form.

Here, the Bitcoin emblem becomes the central expression of the design.

Its scale and positioning allow it to command attention without requiring additional imagery to explain its presence.

GOLD AGAINST BLACK

The colour composition is an essential part of this design's identity.

Gold introduces prominence, warmth and visual richness. Against the surrounding black, it creates a striking contrast that immediately draws the eye towards the central emblem.

The circular golden frame reinforces the composition, while the illuminated orbital lines introduce movement around the symbol.

Small points of golden light appear throughout the artwork, creating an impression of energy and technological activity.

Beneath these elements, subtle circuit-inspired patterns reinforce the connection to the digital world.

The black background provides visual depth, allowing the gold elements to remain the principal focus.

The white body of the tumbler completes the overall presentation, introducing a clean contrast between the printed artwork and the surrounding surface.

Together, these colours create an identity that feels contemporary, confident and visually distinctive.

WHY THIS DESIGN BELONGS ON THE TUMBLER

A tumbler is an object that accompanies people throughout their daily routines.

It moves between workspaces, personal environments, journeys and moments of relaxation.

Its purpose is practical, but that does not mean its appearance must be ordinary.

We saw an opportunity to bring together everyday functionality and a subject that has become increasingly influential in modern culture.

The cylindrical surface provides a defined canvas for the Bitcoin artwork, allowing the central emblem to remain visually prominent.

Rather than distributing multiple competing elements across the design, the composition concentrates attention on one recognisable symbol.

This creates a clear visual identity that can be appreciated from a distance while retaining its finer decorative details upon closer inspection.

The transparent lid and clean white lower body complete the tumbler's practical and visual presentation.

BEYOND THE SYMBOL

Bitcoin has introduced a different way of thinking about financial systems and the exchange of value.

Its emergence has contributed to conversations about decentralisation, digital ownership, financial access and the role of technology in shaping future economic systems.

These conversations are complex, and the future of digital currencies continues to evolve.

Our design does not attempt to reduce those discussions to a simple statement. Instead, it draws inspiration from a symbol that has become part of a much larger cultural and technological transformation.

The distinction is important: appreciating a visual expression of innovation does not require overlooking the complexities surrounding it.

The artwork is an expression of contemporary design inspired by the language of digital finance.

FUNCTION MEETS EXPRESSION

Beyond its visual identity, the tumbler is intended to serve a practical everyday purpose.

Its stainless steel construction provides a durable drinking vessel, while the transparent lid offers a convenient covered drinking design.

The contrasting white body gives the product a clean overall appearance, allowing the darker artwork to stand out without overwhelming the entire composition.

It is an everyday object presented through a distinctive visual perspective: practical in function, contemporary in character and expressive in design.

THE MAISON PERSPECTIVE

At @TENACIOUS, we are interested in the relationship between design, culture and changing expressions of identity.

We recognise that creativity does not exist in isolation. It responds to the world around us, drawing inspiration from technology, history, art, architecture and the ideas that influence contemporary society.

This creation reflects that perspective.

It brings a recognisable symbol of the digital age into an everyday product, giving it a distinctive visual presence.

Through Maison d'Artisanat & Ecosystem, we seek to explore different forms of expression while maintaining a consistent appreciation for intentional design.

We believe that everyday objects can carry an identity of their own.

A FINAL THOUGHT

The meaning of currency has changed throughout history, shaped by innovation, human behaviour and the systems societies establish around exchange.

Today, digital currencies form part of that continuing conversation.

The Bitcoin symbol represents one of the most recognisable expressions of this transformation.

Through this design, we celebrate the visual identity of an idea that has challenged conventional ways of thinking about money and technology.

The world evolves. Ideas transform. Value takes on new expressions.
