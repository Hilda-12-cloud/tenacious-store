A WORLD OF ITS OWN.

Protection Meets Imagination. Every Detail Has a Presence.

An iPhone is something we carry almost everywhere. It holds our conversations, memories, photographs and fragments of our everyday lives. Yet the object that protects it does not have to be ordinary.

This design begins with a bird that seems to have escaped the boundaries of the imagination.

Its wings stretch across the case in a spectacular display of iridescent feathers, shifting between icy blue, violet, lavender and champagne gold. Crystal-like textures and gemstone-inspired accents catch the eye, giving the bird an almost otherworldly presence.

The artwork transforms the back of the iPhone case into a miniature canvas. Rather than hiding the device behind a conventional finish, it gives it a distinct visual identity.

Every time the phone is picked up, the composition reveals itself again. The sweeping wings, intricate details and luminous colours create a sense of movement across a surface designed to be held, seen and carried.

But the bird is more than an ornamental figure.

It suggests freedom, individuality and the courage to stand apart. Its presence turns a practical accessory into a personal expression—one that accompanies you through ordinary moments without ever looking ordinary.

Designed to bring artistic character to everyday protection, this iPhone case places imagination where you can see it, hold it and take it with you.

Because even the smallest things we carry can say something about who we are.

A protective essential. An unexpected work of art.
