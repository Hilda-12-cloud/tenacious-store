BEYOND WHAT THE EYES CAN SEE.

Every Vision Begins Within.

Product: Laptop Sleeve.

There is a difference between looking at the world and truly seeing it.

One requires sight. The other requires perspective.

This design brings together the human eye and an imagined landscape, creating a visual composition where nature, architecture and the elements meet.

At its centre is an eye with a fiery, amber-toned iris. Its intensity draws attention immediately, while the surrounding imagery creates a world within the gaze.

The solitary tree introduces a striking contrast against the distant city skyline. It stands between the natural and the constructed, the quiet and the restless, the familiar and the unknown.

Beyond it, the city rises against a dramatic sunset, its reflection stretching across the water. The combination of orange, gold, blue and black gives the artwork its depth, atmosphere and cinematic character.

The eye becomes more than a focal point. It invites us to consider perception: how we interpret our surroundings, how we imagine possibilities and how our experiences shape the way we see the world.

At @Tenacious, we believe that vision extends beyond what is immediately visible. It is found in the ability to imagine something greater, recognise possibilities where others see limitations, and remain determined about the direction we choose.

Because the world we see is influenced by the vision we carry.

Look beyond the obvious. See beyond the familiar.
