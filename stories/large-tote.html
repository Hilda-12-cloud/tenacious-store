BEYOND THE HOURS.

Every Journey Leaves a Story.
Product: Large All-Over Print Tote Bag.

Time moves in one direction, yet every individual experiences it differently.

Some moments become defining memories. Others become lessons, turning points or experiences that quietly influence the person we eventually become.

We measure time through hours, days and years, but the meaning of our existence cannot be measured by a clock alone.

It is found in the places we encounter, the decisions we make, the paths we take and the experiences we carry along the way.

This is the thinking behind BEYOND THE HOURS.

Rather than approaching the tote as an ordinary everyday accessory, we envisioned it as a canvas for two contrasting visual expressions connected by a shared idea: the passage of time and the journey through life.

The two exterior artworks present different atmospheres, yet together they invite reflection on something universal.

Time passes. Journeys unfold. Experiences become part of our personal history.

THE FRONT: THE ARCHITECTURE OF TIME

The front artwork introduces a dramatic interpretation of time through an oversized antique clock.

Its prominent Roman numerals and imposing clock structure establish the central visual theme, drawing attention to the measurement and passage of time.

Surrounding the clock is an atmospheric architectural landscape, featuring weathered stone-like structures, distant Gothic buildings and a sky filled with stormy charcoal clouds.

Fiery orange and amber tones emerge through the darkness, creating a striking contrast against the cooler grey and blue atmosphere.

The composition feels almost suspended between different moments: the permanence suggested by the ancient architecture and the continuous movement represented by the clock.

The Roman numerals introduce a classical visual language, while the dramatic surroundings give the artwork a cinematic character.

We find this contrast particularly compelling. Time is universal, but the way we experience it is deeply personal.

The artwork serves as a visual reminder that every hour belongs to a larger story.

THE BACK: THE JOURNEY THROUGH LIFE

Turning to the opposite side reveals an entirely different atmosphere.

Where the front is dominated by architectural drama and the imagery of an antique clock, the back presents a winding river illuminated by a warm, golden-orange glow.

The water moves through a dark surrounding landscape, creating a natural pathway that draws the eye towards the illuminated distance.

Along the riverbanks, delicate white flowers introduce a softer visual element. Their presence contrasts beautifully with the surrounding darkness and the warmth of the reflected light.

The river becomes an interesting metaphor for the journey of life.

Its winding course suggests that our journeys are rarely perfectly straight. There are unexpected turns, changing surroundings and destinations that become visible only as we continue forward.

The illuminated water introduces a sense of direction and possibility, while the flowers offer a quieter expression of beauty within the surrounding landscape.

It is a composition that encourages a different kind of reflection: not simply how much time has passed, but what we choose to do with the time we have.

TWO ARTWORKS. ONE NARRATIVE.

The relationship between the two sides is what gives this design its distinctive editorial character.

The front explores the passage of time through the imagery of an antique clock. The back explores the movement of life through the imagery of a winding river.

One presents time as something we measure. The other presents life as something we experience.

Together, they create a visual dialogue between time and existence.

We wanted both sides to have their own identity rather than simply repeating the same artwork.

This distinction allows the tote to reveal different visual experiences depending on which side is presented.

The contrast between the dark architectural drama of the front and the warm natural illumination of the back also creates an interesting balance between intensity and tranquillity.

Neither side tells the entire story alone. Their relationship is what completes the composition.

THE SIGNATURE WITHIN

The exterior artwork is only one part of the tote's identity.

Inside, a contrasting white pocket introduces a clean visual interruption against the black interior.

Positioned prominently on the pocket is the @Tenacious wordmark, presented in black lettering.

This interior detail establishes a direct connection between the artistic expression on the outside and the identity of the Maison.

It also introduces a more personal brand presence, one that becomes visible when the bag is opened.

The black interior, contrasting white pocket and branded presentation create a distinct visual relationship within the construction.

Beyond its appearance, the interior pocket provides a practical space for keeping smaller belongings organised.

It is an important part of the overall design, bringing together everyday functionality and brand identity.

DESIGNED FOR THE JOURNEY

A tote bag is naturally associated with movement.

It accompanies us through different environments, daily responsibilities, personal errands and spontaneous experiences.

It carries the things we need while becoming part of the routines that shape our everyday lives.

We found this relationship between the purpose of the tote and the meaning behind its artwork particularly fitting.

A design exploring time and life's journey belongs naturally on an accessory that moves alongside its owner.

The spacious silhouette provides room for everyday essentials, while the all-over artwork gives the bag a strong visual identity.

Its black webbing handles complement the darker elements of the artwork, helping to maintain visual continuity throughout the overall presentation.

This is an accessory intended to be both useful and expressive.

THE MAISON PERSPECTIVE

At @TENACIOUS, we believe that design can communicate ideas beyond its immediate function.

An image can introduce a conversation. A composition can evoke an emotion. A product can become an expression of an idea that extends beyond the object itself.

This tote reflects our appreciation for visual storytelling and the relationship between artistic expression and everyday objects.

We are interested in creations that offer more than a surface appearance: pieces with a distinctive visual identity and a narrative worth exploring.

Through Maison d'Artisanat & Ecosystem, we seek to bring together artistic interpretation, purposeful design and individual expression.

The Large Tote Bag is one interpretation of that vision.

A FINAL THOUGHT

We cannot return to the hours that have passed, nor can we predict every turn our journeys will take.

What we can do is recognise the value of the present, remain conscious of the choices before us and give meaning to the experiences that shape our lives.

Time gives our experiences their place in history. Our journeys give those experiences their meaning.

And somewhere between the hours we measure and the paths we travel, our stories continue to unfold.

Time moves. Journeys unfold. Make every moment part of your story.
